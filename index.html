💡 Range Analysis

📖 HUD & Exploit Cheat Sheet

Key HUD Stats

VPIP
Voluntarily put $ in pot. High = loose. Low = tight.
PFR
Preflop raise %. Gap vs VPIP = limp/call-heavy, passive.
3-Bet %
How often they re-raise preflop. Low = only value.
WTSD
Went to showdown %. High = calling station profile.
AFq
Aggression frequency postflop. Low = passive, folds a lot.
Fold to C-bet
High = steal/bluff more. Low = value bet more, bluff less.

Population Tendencies by Stake

2NL–25NLLimp-heavy, call too much, rarely bluff. Value bet big & thin, cut bluffs, iso-raise limpers hard.
50NL–100NLRegs appear. Default GTO-ish vs unknowns, exploit tagged recreational players, 3-bet bluffs become viable.
Live 1/2–2/5Passive, hate folding top pair, big live tells. Overbet rivers for value, bluff rarely vs unknowns.
Live 5/10+Capable regs. Balance more, watch bet-sizing & timing tells closely.

Player Archetypes

StationCalls too much, folds too little → value bet relentlessly, never bluff.
Nit / RockFolds to any pressure → steal liberally, fold instantly to their raises.
ManiacRaises/bluffs constantly → tighten up, let them bluff into your value, trap.
Thinking RegBalances & adjusts → shift toward GTO, take small edges, don't over-exploit.

Bet-Sizing Quick Reference

25–33% potDry/paired boards, range bets, cheap protection.
50–66% potStandard c-bet, medium-wet boards, balanced value/bluff.
75–125% potWet boards, polarized value/bluff, denying draws.
Overbet 125%+Nutted river spots vs overfolders, huge range advantage.
